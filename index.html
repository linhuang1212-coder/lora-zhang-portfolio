About

Lora Zhang

Born in China and trained in the ateliers of Paris, Lora Zhang brings an East-meets-West sensibility to her jewelry design practice. Her work merges the fluid elegance of Chinese jade-carving traditions with the precision of European haute joaillerie.

Each collection begins as a dialogue between material and meaning — exploring the tension between organic forms and architectural geometry, between light captured and light released. Her pieces have been featured in Vogue China, Harper's Bazaar, and Wallpaper*, and are held in private collections across Asia and Europe.

Lora maintains her atelier in Shanghai, where she works with a small team of master artisans to realize both her seasonal collections and bespoke commissions for private clients and maisons.
